💪why your body need exercise

Exercise is important for kids because it helps them build strong bones and muscles, maintain a healthy weight, and improve their overall physical and mental health. Regular physical activity can also help reduce the risk of developing chronic diseases later in life.

Exercise strengthens muscles and bones, improves cardiovascular health, and helps maintain a healthy weight. Engaging in regular physical activity reduces the risk of heart disease, stroke, type 2 diabetes, and certain cancers, while also improving blood pressure, cholesterol levels, and blood sugar control Centers for Disease Control and Prevention Centers for Disease Control and Prevention +3 . Strength training and weight-bearing exercises help prevent osteoporosis and injuries, especially as we age Healthline Healthline +1 . Even moderate activity, like walking or household chores, contributes to these benefits
